The result is a refreshed space that feels polished, functional, and uniquely yours.

Design Consultation Package

Whether you're redesigning a home or reimagining a business space, this service offers flexible, in-depth support tailored to your goals. It's ideal for those tackling a remodel, launching a new location, or feeling creatively stuck. Together, we'll develop a cohesive plan that reflects your brand or lifestyle, with thoughtful attention to layout, function, and aesthetics. You'll handle the implementation and day-to-day project management, while I provide the creative direction and expertise to guide the process. Scope and hours are customized based on your needs and budget.

Starting at $1200 / 12hr Minimum / Project Dependant

Full Service Design Package

This is a comprehensive, start-to-finish design experience for clients ready to hand off the process and see their vision fully brought to life. From initial mood boards to final styling, I'll lead every step — managing the project, sourcing and selecting materials, overseeing implementation, and handling all the details in between. Whether it's one room or an entire home, this service is tailored to deliver a cohesive, beautifully executed space. All you have to do is enjoy the result.
