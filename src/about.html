About Tri Cities Design Co.

Tri‑Cities Design Co. blends creativity and functionality to deliver refined design solutions across interior, exterior, retail, and civic spaces. We are rooted in intentional design and regional collaboration.

Meet the Designer

Megan Thayer

Lead Interior Designer

Megan is an interior designer dedicated to creating spaces that are both beautiful and highly functional. Her work spans a range of projects, from reimagining a kitchen to maximize efficiency and style, to reorganizing a living room for improved flow and comfort. She has also designed striking windowscapes for a flower company, blending color, texture, and composition to highlight their products in an inviting way. With a thoughtful approach and strong attention to detail, Megan helps clients transform their spaces into environments that reflect their personality and needs.
